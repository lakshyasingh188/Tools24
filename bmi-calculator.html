BMI Calculator for Indian Users

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. This tool provides both standard WHO and specific Indian/Asian BMI reference ranges.

How to Calculate BMI

BMI is calculated using the formula: BMI = weight (kg) / height² (m). Our calculator handles unit conversions automatically.
