Is Tools24 Background Remover free?

Yes. Tools24 Background Remover is completely free to use. You can remove backgrounds from your images without any cost, signup or watermark.

Which image formats are supported?

The Tools24 background remover supports JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP image formats for upload. The processed result can be downloaded as a transparent PNG or as a JPG with a white (or custom) background.

Can I download a transparent PNG?

Yes. Once the AI successfully removes the background, you can download the result as a transparent PNG image with the transparency preserved.

Does the tool work on mobile?

Yes. The Tools24 Background Remover interface is fully responsive and works smoothly on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Does background removal reduce image quality?

The implementation preserves the original resolution as much as reasonably possible. The output PNG maintains high quality suitable for e-commerce, profile pictures and design use.

Can I remove the background from product photos?

Yes. The AI background removal engine works well with product photos for e-commerce platforms like Amazon and Flipkart, objects and catalog images.

Can I remove the background from a person's photo?

Yes. Subject detection works with common portrait images including profile pictures, passport-style photos and full body portraits.
