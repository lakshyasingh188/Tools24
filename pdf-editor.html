Tools24 PDF Editor – Free Online PDF Editor

Tools24 PDF Editor is a free, browser-based PDF editing tool that lets you edit PDF files online without installing any software. Add text, images, signatures, highlights, drawings and shapes directly on your PDF pages, organize pages, and download the edited file — all in your browser. Your files are processed locally whenever possible, so sensitive documents never leave your device.

How to Edit a PDF Online

  1. Upload your PDF — drag and drop your file or click "Choose PDF".
  2. Choose an editing tool — text, image, draw, highlight, shapes, signature or whiteout.
  3. Edit your document — click on the page to add content, drag to move, use handles to resize and rotate.
  4. Review your changes — zoom, navigate pages, organize and adjust positions.
  5. Download the edited PDF — click Download to save your final file.

What Can You Do With Tools24 PDF Editor?

  • Add text to any page with custom fonts, sizes, colors, alignment and styles.
  • Add images — insert JPG, PNG or WebP images, resize, move and rotate them.
  • Sign PDF — draw, type or upload your signature and place it anywhere.
  • Highlight PDF — mark content in yellow, green, blue or pink.
  • Draw on PDF — freehand drawing with custom stroke, color and opacity.
  • Add shapes — rectangle, circle, line and arrow.
  • Whiteout — cover unwanted content with a solid fill, then add replacement text.
  • Organize pages — reorder, duplicate, delete and rotate pages.
  • Download edited PDF — export as a fresh, self-contained PDF file.
