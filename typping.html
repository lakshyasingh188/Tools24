      What is a Typing Test?

      A typing test is a tool that measures how fast and accurately you can type on a keyboard. It records your typing speed in Words Per Minute (WPM), Characters Per Minute (CPM), and your overall accuracy percentage. Typing tests are widely used by students, job seekers, and professionals in India to assess and improve their keyboard skills for academic, competitive exam, and office work purposes.

      How is WPM Calculated?

      WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated using a standard formula accepted worldwide:

      WPM = (Total Characters Typed ÷ 5) ÷ Time in Minutes

      The number 5 represents the average length of a word in English. So if you type 250 characters in 1 minute, your WPM would be (250 ÷ 5) ÷ 1 = 50 WPM. This standardization allows fair comparison regardless of word lengths in the passage.

      What is a Good Typing Speed?

      Typing speed varies by experience level. Here are general benchmarks:

      • Below 20 WPM: Beginner level — just starting to learn typing
      • 20–35 WPM: Below average — typical for occasional computer users
      • 35–50 WPM: Average — suitable for most everyday tasks
      • 50–70 WPM: Above average — good for office and data entry work
      • 70–90 WPM: Fast — excellent for professional typing
      • Above 90 WPM: Very fast — expert level typist

      Remember that accuracy is just as important as speed. A slower but accurate typist is often more valuable than a fast but error-prone one.

      How to Improve Typing Speed

      • Practice regularly: Consistent daily practice of 15–30 minutes yields the best results
      • Learn touch typing: Type without looking at the keyboard using all ten fingers
      • Maintain proper posture: Sit straight, keep wrists flat, and position fingers on the home row
      • Focus on accuracy first: Speed comes naturally once you type accurately
      • Use the right finger positions: Left hand on ASDF, right hand on JKL;
      • Take breaks: Rest your hands every 20–30 minutes to prevent strain
      • Track your progress: Use our history feature to monitor improvement over time

      WPM vs CPM

      WPM (Words Per Minute) and CPM (Characters Per Minute) are related but different metrics:

      • CPM counts every individual character you type, including spaces and punctuation
      • WPM divides the character count by 5 (the average word length) to estimate words typed
      • The formula is: WPM = CPM ÷ 5
      • CPM gives a raw measure of keystroke speed, while WPM provides a more intuitive, standardized measure

      Most typing tests and job requirements use WPM as the standard metric, but CPM is useful for detailed performance analysis.
