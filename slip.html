Salary Slip Generator India

A salary slip, also known as a payslip, is a vital document issued by employers to their employees every month. It details the employee's earnings, deductions, and net payable salary. With Tools24's India-focused Salary Slip Generator, HR professionals and small business owners can create accurate, professional payslips in minutes without needing expensive software.

Our tool ensures privacy by processing all data locally in your browser. You can add company details, employee information, custom allowances, and deductions (PF, ESI, PT, TDS), then download the final slip as a PDF or PNG.

How to Create a Salary Slip

  1. Enter your company details, including name, address, and logo.
  2. Fill in the employee details like name, ID, designation, and department.
  3. Select the salary month and enter attendance details.
  4. Add all earning components (Basic, HRA, Allowances) and their amounts.
  5. Add deduction components (PF, ESI, TDS, etc.).
  6. Check the auto-calculated Net Salary and salary in words.
  7. Preview the salary slip to ensure everything is correct.
  8. Download as PDF, PNG, or print it directly in A4 Landscape.

Frequently Asked Questions

A salary slip is a document issued by an employer to an employee detailing the amount of money earned, deductions made, and the final net payable salary for a specific period, usually a month.
Gross salary is the total amount of money earned by an employee before any deductions like tax, PF, or ESI are taken out. It includes basic pay, HRA, allowances, and bonuses.
Net salary, or take-home pay, is the amount an employee actually receives after all deductions (PF, ESI, TDS, PT) have been subtracted from the gross salary.
Basic salary is the base income of an employee, which is usually fixed and comprises a significant portion of the total salary. Other components like HRA are often calculated as a percentage of the basic salary.
House Rent Allowance (HRA) is a component of the salary provided by employers to employees to meet the cost of rented accommodation. It offers tax benefits under the Income Tax Act.
Provident Fund (PF) or EPF is a retirement benefit scheme. Both the employer and employee contribute a certain percentage (usually 12%) of the basic salary to the PF account.
Employees' State Insurance (ESI) is a social security and health insurance scheme for Indian workers. It is applicable to employees earning below a certain wage threshold.
Professional Tax is a tax levied by state governments on professionals and employees. The amount varies from state to state and is deducted from the employee's salary.
Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is the income tax deducted by the employer from the employee's salary and deposited with the government. It depends on the employee's tax slab and regime.
