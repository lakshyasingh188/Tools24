Photo Resizer Online

The Tools24 Photo Resizer is a free, browser-based image resizing tool that lets you change the dimensions, quality, and format of your images in seconds. Whether you need to compress a photo for email, prepare images for social media, or convert between formats like JPG, PNG, and WEBP, this tool handles everything directly in your browser without uploading your files to any server.

Because all processing happens locally using the HTML5 Canvas API, your images remain 100% private. There are no uploads, no queues, and no waiting — just instant, high-quality image resizing on any device.

How to Resize an Image

  1. Select your image by clicking the upload box or dragging and dropping a file.
  2. Enter your desired width and height, choose a percentage, or pick a preset.
  3. Adjust the image quality slider if you want to compress the file size.
  4. Select your preferred output format — JPG, PNG, or WEBP.
  5. Use the rotate, flip, and crop tools if you need to edit your image first.
  6. Click the "Resize Image" button and wait for processing to complete.
  7. Download your resized image instantly to your device.

For most web use cases, a quality of 80–85% offers an excellent balance between visual quality and file size. If you need maximum quality for printing or archiving, use 95–100%. For thumbnails or previews, 60–70% is usually sufficient.
Downscaling an image generally preserves quality well. Upscaling beyond the original resolution will result in some pixelation because the tool cannot create detail that was not in the original image. Use the quality slider to control compression for JPG and WEBP outputs.

JPG is best for photographs with small file sizes but no transparency. PNG supports transparency and is ideal for graphics and logos but produces larger files.
