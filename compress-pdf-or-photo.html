Image & PDF Compressor Online

Tools24 Image & PDF Compressor is a powerful browser-based tool that allows you to reduce the file size of your images and PDF documents quickly and securely. Whether you need to meet email attachment limits, upload files to a web portal, or simply save storage space, our compressor provides the perfect solution without compromising on quality.

Because all processing happens locally in your browser using advanced HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly technologies, your files are never uploaded to a server. This ensures 100% privacy and instantaneous processing speeds.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Select an image by dragging and dropping it onto the upload area or clicking "Select File".
  2. Choose your desired target file size (e.g., 100 KB) or switch to Manual Quality mode.
  3. Select your preferred output format: JPG, PNG, or WEBP.
  4. If needed, enable the "Resize Before Compression" option to change image dimensions.
  5. Click the "Compress Image" button and wait for the process to finish.
  6. Compare the original and compressed sizes, then click "Download File".

How to Compress a PDF

  1. Select the PDF Compressor tab at the top of the tool.
  2. Upload your PDF file using the drag-and-drop area or file selector.
  3. Review the automatically generated page thumbnails and remove any unwanted pages.
  4. Select a compression level: High Quality, Balanced, or Maximum Compression.
  5. Choose the desired DPI (Dots Per Inch) for the output PDF.
  6. Click "Compress PDF" and download your optimized document.
