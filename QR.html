What Is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that can be scanned using a smartphone camera. Unlike traditional barcodes that only hold numbers, QR codes can store URLs, text, contact information, Wi-Fi credentials, payment details, and much more. They were invented in 1994 by Denso Wave in Japan and have become essential for digital payments, marketing, and contactless information sharing.

In India, QR codes are everywhere — from UPI payment displays at kirana stores to restaurant menus, digital visiting cards, and event registrations. The Tools24 QR Code Generator makes it easy to create your own professional QR codes for any of these purposes.

How to Create a UPI QR Code

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) QR codes are widely used across India for digital payments. With the Tools24 UPI QR Code Generator, shops, restaurants, and small businesses can create payment QR codes for free.

  1. Select the UPI tab in the QR type selector.
  2. Enter your UPI ID (e.g., yourname@upi, yourname@okaxis, yourname@ybl).
  3. Enter your Payee Name as registered with your UPI app.
  4. Optionally enter an Amount and Transaction Note.
  5. Click Download to save your UPI QR code.

When customers scan this QR code using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.), the payment details auto-fill. Always verify your UPI details before sharing or printing your QR code.

How to Download a QR Code

The Tools24 QR Code Generator supports four download formats:

  • PNG: Best for general use. Transparent background not supported (use SVG for that).
  • JPG: Smallest file size. Good for email and web.
  • WebP: Modern format with excellent compression and quality.
  • SVG: True vector format. Scales infinitely without quality loss. Best for printing.

For printing, use SVG or a high-resolution PNG (1024px or 2048px). For web use, PNG or WebP at 512px is usually sufficient.

QR Code Safety Tips

  • Test before printing: Always scan your QR code with multiple devices before printing or sharing.
  • Maintain contrast: Use dark foreground colors on light backgrounds for best scanability.
  • Don't make QR codes too small: For print, ensure the QR code is at least 2cm × 2cm.
  • Use appropriate error correction: Use level H (High) when adding a logo or printing on materials that may get damaged.
  • Keep the quiet zone: Maintain white space around the QR code for reliable scanning.
  • Verify UPI details: Always double-check UPI IDs and payee names before sharing payment QR codes.
  • Use HTTPS URLs: For URL QR codes, always use https:// links for security.
