Passport Photo Maker Features

Tools24 Passport Photo Maker lets you create a passport-size photo directly in your browser — no software installation, no signup, and no upload to any server. Upload a photo or capture one with your camera, choose the size you need, adjust the crop and background, then download a high-quality image or a ready-to-print photo sheet.

How to Make a Passport Size Photo?

  1. Upload or capture your photo.
  2. Select your country's preset size or enter a custom size.
  3. Crop and adjust brightness/contrast.
  4. Change the background to white or blue.
  5. Download as JPG/PNG or generate a printable A4 PDF sheet.
